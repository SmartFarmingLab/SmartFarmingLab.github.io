What we do

From real field data to deployable autonomy

Agricultural machines work in conditions that urban and off-road datasets do not cover: implements in view, changing soil states, dust, headlands and cluttered farmyards. LOAM records, annotates and simulates exactly these situations.

Datasets & Research

LOAM-D, the first large-scale semantic segmentation dataset for tractors in farmyards and fields, plus peer-reviewed perception methods.
