Full Perception Integration

A complete perception stack on a working tractor

Our Fendt 724 Vario carries a rugged, retrofittable roof-bar sensor kit and records continuously during plowing, tilling, cultivating, mowing and baling on the research farm of Leipzig University (Lehr- und Forschungsgut Oberholz).

Series machine

Real operations on a series-production tractor, not a prototype vehicle.

Safety-relevant

Traversability, people and obstacle detection with 360° camera coverage and LiDAR.

Embedded real-time

Pipelines run on board an NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin, e.g. windrow detection at more than 20 Hz.

Interoperable

A GOOSE-compatible ontology lets you combine our data with existing off-road datasets.

Sensor kit & compute

  • ZED 2i stereo camera (front)
  • 5x ArkVision Basic+ Mini 120° RGB cameras
  • Ouster OS0-128 LiDAR (front) and Blickfeld Qb2 LiDAR (rear)
  • NovAtel Smart7/Smart2 GNSS/INS with RTK/NTRIP
  • NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin
  • Teltonika RUTX50 5G router
  • Customized MittX roof bar with 10x Hella Black Magic Cube LED lights

Simulation

Digital twin in NVIDIA Isaac Sim

Our tractor, its sensor roof bar and the surrounding fields are replicated in NVIDIA Isaac Sim. This lets us test perception across crop growth stages and rare, safety-critical situations before going to the field.

Full sensor simulation

Cameras, stereo, LiDAR and GNSS/INS are simulated with the same mounting positions as on the real roof bar.

Scenario variation

Vary crop type and growth stage and place people, animals or obstacles in the field – including situations too risky to stage for real.

Hardware-in-the-loop

Simulated sensor streams feed the onboard compute, so the perception stack is tested on the target hardware before field trials.
